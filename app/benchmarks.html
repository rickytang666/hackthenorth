Methodology & scope

Cohere uses supervised audio/transcript fine-tuning with rank-8 LoRA (alpha 16, dropout 0.05), targeting the final six encoder blocks and all eight decoder layers. Base weights remain frozen. Training ran for 1,200 steps; validation loss selected step 900.

Parakeet uses supervised speech adaptation with dim-32 encoder adapters and greedy decoding. The partial arms also update the final two encoder blocks. Different model architectures use different training implementations; each table compares results within its stated evaluation protocol.

TORGO contains read prompts and isolated words. Small held-out sets and training-speaker results do not establish performance on spontaneous speech. The reported out-of-domain clip did not improve after adaptation.
