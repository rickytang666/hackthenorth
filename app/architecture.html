How it works

1. CaptureBrowser sends 20 ms PCM frames over a WebSocket.
2. RecoverLoRA-adapted Cohere Transcribe returns a revised transcript every 400 ms.
3. ScoreAbove 0.879 confidence, accept. Below, ask.
4. SpeakMeloTTS generates the sentence, OpenVoice V2 repaints it in the speaker's voice.

Offline, once

TORGO dysarthric speech adapted two candidate recognizers. Cohere won the only matched comparison, so it is the one the live path calls.

Two decisions

Only the encoder is adapted. Dysarthria is acoustic, and the encoder is 91.8% of the parameters. 3.4M weights trained, 0.166% of the model.

Voice conversion, not cloning. Cloning a dysarthric reference would reproduce the slurring. OpenVoice transfers timbre only.
