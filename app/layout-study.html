Adapting speech recognition to dysarthric speech

A compact result from one sealed speaker, presented alongside the limitations of the evaluation.

The frozen speech model produced a word error rate of 56.7% across 388 clips from speaker M02. After adaptation, word error fell to 34.8%, a relative reduction of 38.6%.

On the selected recording, the baseline transcript was “who were killed.” The adapted transcript was “wicked,” matching the reference.
